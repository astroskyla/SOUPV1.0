# Future Innovations

This section outlines the planned expansions and improvements for SOUP, focusing on enhancing the database and modeling capabilities.

---

## Database Enhancements

- **Expand Reaction Coverage**: Increase the database's scope by including a wider variety of reactions.
- **Data Validation**: Ensure all entries are describe din terms of their associated errors to maintain accuracy and reliability.
- **Environmental Scaling Factors**: Introduce scaling factors for rate constants that change based on external conditions such as temperature and pressure.
- **Expand Species Properties**: Include more information of chemical species including absoprtion cross-sections, densities, and activity coefficients.

## Model Improvements

- **Depth-Dependent Solar Spectrum**: Integration of a depth-dependent solar spectrum in aqueous environments that uses the absorption cross-sections of various UV-absorbers.
- **Temperature Profile Integration**: Addition of a temperature profile and functions for the dynamic adustment of rate constants.
- **Mixing**: Incorporation of a mixing routine that accounts for species density.
- **Units**: A function for dealing with alternative units.
