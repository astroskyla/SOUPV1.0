# Welcome to SOUP

---

**SOUP** is designed to facilitate collaboration by enabling researchers to identify and prioritise the most promising environments for investigation based on the productivity of a reaction network under a given set of conditions.

!!! quote "Quote"
    “A high fidelity picture of multiple prebiotic scenarios, at the level of chemical kinetics, will not be a task for a single group.” White & Rimmer, *Accounts of Chemical Research.* 2025.

---

## Key Features

### The Database

Our [database](Database.md#database) aims to enhance the quantitative aspects of origin of life research by providing extensive data for researchers to use. Although at present this database is relatively small and incomplete it holds significant potential to integrate data in unprecedented ways. To realise this vision, the SOUP tool requires **YOUR** contribution. To find out how you can contribute click [here](Database.md#database)

### The Model
The [model](Model.md#the-soup-model) leverages inputs from the database to assess the viability of a prebiotic reaction network in a given environment.

---

## How It Works

**1. Data Collection**

- Add rreactions and their corresponding kinetic data to the SOUP Database.

**2. Data Analysis**

- Choose (or create) a reaction network and an environment, then simulate how the system evolves over time.

**3. Simulations**

- Analyse the simulation results to uncover insights on the most promising environments for further investigation.
